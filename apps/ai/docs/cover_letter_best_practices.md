## 🎯 Patterns identifiés

### Longueur & Format

**Finance (Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan):**
- **250-400 mots** (cible: ~350 mots)
- **1 page A4** exactement
- **4-5 paragraphes** courts et percutants
- Temps lecture: **30 secondes max** (scanning rapide)
- Marges: **20-25mm** (plus aérées que CV)

**Conseil (McKinsey, BCG, Bain):**
- **250-400 mots** (cible: ~320 mots)
- **3-4 paragraphes** ultra-denses
- **Bullet points** parfois utilisés pour achievements
- Temps lecture: **10-30 secondes** (screening initial)

**Consensus:**
✅ **1 page exactement**
✅ **250-400 mots** (sweet spot: 300-350 mots)
✅ **4-5 paragraphes** (finance) ou **3-4** (conseil)
✅ **Scan-friendly** (recruiter lit en diagonale)

---

### Structure - Investment Banking (Goldman Sachs)

**§1: Introduction + Hook (40-80 mots)**
- Qui tu es + pourquoi tu écris
- **Hook:** Biggest selling point immédiatement
- ❌ Éviter: "I'm writing to express my interest..."
- ✅ Préférer: Lead avec achievement ou insight entreprise

**§2: Sell Yourself (80-120 mots)**
- **Personnalité + Skills match**
- **2-3 achievements quantifiés** (chiffres, %, deals)
- Éviter phrases génériques
- Exemples: "Increased sales by 20%", "Led $2M deal"

**§3: Why This Industry/Division (80-120 mots)**
- Passion pour l'industrie
- Link skills → motivation spécifique (M&A, equity, etc.)
- Job-relevant achievements dans ce domaine

**§4: Why Goldman Sachs (50-80 mots)**
- **Recherche approfondie** (NOT copy/paste site web)
- Mentions spécifiques: training model, team, clients, tech depth
- **Cultural fit:** mentorship, client-first, ethics, resilience
- **Différenciation:** Pourquoi GS et pas JPM/MS?

**§5: Closing (40-60 mots)**
- Disponibilité entretien
- Remerciements formels
- CTA: "I look forward to discussing..."

**Word count example (Wall Street Oasis):**
- §1: 75 mots (école + impressed by company)
- §2: 120 mots (academic + work experience)
- §3: 120 mots (leadership)
- §4: 50 mots (look forward to interview)
- **Total: ~350 mots**

---

### Structure - Management Consulting (McKinsey/BCG)

**§1: Value Proposition (50-70 mots)**
- Interest pour rôle spécifique + firm
- **1-2 sentence value prop** (ta thèse unique)

**§2-4: Demonstrate Value (240-300 mots total)**
- **2-3 stories** avec bullet points
- **Metrics obligatoires:** "Increased X by Y% over Z months"
- Démontre: problem-solving, impact, leadership
- ⚠️ **Règle:** Si tu peux remplacer "BCG" par "McKinsey" et ça marche, pas assez spécifique

**§5: Why This Firm (50-70 mots)**
- **Ultra-spécifique:** BCG X, Henderson Institute, creative problem framing
- Mention conversation avec consultant BCG (networking)
- ❌ Pas de platitudes génériques

**§6: Closing (30-50 mots)**
- Polite, concis, CTA clair

**Key Insight (CareerInConsulting):**
> "Claims without numbers are claims without credibility."
> "Cover letters with 2-3 specific metrics advance at significantly higher rate."

**Fatal Error Example:**
> Finir lettre McKinsey avec "...and this is why I want to work at BCG" → **Instant rejection**

## 🎨 Ton & Style

### Finance (Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan)

**Ton:**
- **Formel mais pas robotique**
- Confident sans arrogance
- Passion mesurée (pas over-enthusiastic)
- Data-driven (chiffres > adjectifs)

**Formules types:**
- Opening: "As a [background] with proven success in [domain], I am writing to..."
- Achievements: "I delivered [result] by [method], resulting in [impact]"
- Closing: "I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how my experience aligns with Goldman Sachs' strategic priorities."

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Répéter le CV (lettre = storytelling, pas summary)
- ❌ Bland phrases ("team player", "hard worker")
- ❌ Typos (instant reject)
- ❌ Trop long (>400 mots = red flag)

---

### Consulting (McKinsey, BCG, Bain)

**Ton:**
- **Ultra-concis** (chaque mot compte)
- Analytique + storytelling
- Démontrer structured thinking
- Client impact focus

**Formules types:**
- Value prop: "My experience [X] + skills [Y] position me to deliver [Z] impact at BCG"
- Achievement: "Led team of 5 to redesign supply chain, reducing costs 18% ($2.4M annually)"
- Why firm: "BCG's emphasis on creative problem framing, as discussed with [Consultant Name], aligns with my approach to..."

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Generic ("I want to solve complex problems") → Tous les candidats disent ça
- ❌ Pas de metrics (claims non quantifiés)
- ❌ Copy/paste entre firms
- ❌ >400 mots (screening = 30 sec max)

## 🏗️ Guidelines Postulae

### Contraintes strictes

**Format:**
- **1 page A4** (210 x 297 mm)
- **Marges:** 20mm top/bottom, 25mm left/right
- **Font:** Times New Roman, 11pt body, 12pt header
- **Line-height:** 1.4-1.5 (aéré, pas compressé comme CV)
- **Export:** PDF (Playwright) + DOCX

**Longueur:**
- **Target: 300-350 mots** (zone optimale)
- **Min: 250 mots** (trop court = manque contenu)
- **Max: 400 mots** (trop long = perte attention)
- **Paragraphes:** 4-5 (finance) ou 3-4 (conseil)

**Structure obligatoire:**
1. **Header** (réutilisé du CV: nom, contact)
2. **§1 Hook** (50-70 mots): Pourquoi ce poste + value prop
3. **§2-3 Fit** (160-200 mots): 2 achievements CV alignés job
4. **§4 Why Company** (50-70 mots): Recherche entreprise spécifique
5. **§5 Closing** (40-60 mots): Disponibilité, CTA entretien

---

### Règles contenu

**INTERDICTIONS:**
- ❌ **ZÉRO invention** (facts du CV uniquement)
- ❌ Skills non mentionnés dans CV
- ❌ Achievements exagérés
- ❌ Copy/paste site web entreprise
- ❌ Formules génériques ("passionate team player")

**OBLIGATIONS:**
- ✅ **2-3 metrics quantifiés** minimum
- ✅ **Nom entreprise 2-3×** (personnalisation)
- ✅ **Action verbs** (Led, Delivered, Increased, Optimized)
- ✅ **Cultural fit signals** (valeurs alignées)
- ✅ **Specific team/division** mention si connu

---

### Ton selon langue

**Français:**
- Vouvoiement strict
- "Madame, Monsieur" (si destinataire inconnu)
- "Chère Madame X" (si nom connu)
- Formules closing: "Je vous prie d'agréer, Madame, Monsieur, l'expression de mes salutations distinguées."
- Ton formel mais pas archaïque

**Anglais:**
- "Dear Hiring Manager" (si inconnu)
- "Dear Mr./Ms. [Name]" (si connu)
- Formules closing: "Sincerely," ou "Best regards,"
- Ton légèrement plus direct que français
- Pas de vouvoiement excessif

- ✅ Achievements du CV (pas invention): +10
- ✅ Why company research: +5

- ✅ Formel sans robotique: +10
- ✅ Action verbs (5+ occurrences): +5
- ✅ Pas de phrases génériques: +5

- ✅ 1 page exacte: +5

## 🚨 Red Flags (Instant Reject)

1. **>1 page** ou **<250 mots**
2. **Typos** ou fautes grammaire
3. **Wrong company name** (copié-collé)
4. **Generic** ("I'm a passionate team player who...")
5. **No metrics** (aucun chiffre)
6. **CV summary** (répète CV sans storytelling)
7. **No research** (platitudes site web entreprise)
8. **Trop long** (>400 mots = candidat bavard)

## 📚 Exemples annotés

### Example 1: Goldman Sachs Investment Banking Analyst

**§1 (60 mots):**
> As a Finance graduate from HEC Paris with hands-on M&A experience, I am writing to apply for the Investment Banking Analyst position at Goldman Sachs. Having analyzed 15+ cross-border deals during my internship at Rothschild, I am drawn to Goldman's leadership in complex transactions and its commitment to intellectual rigor.

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Background (HEC Paris)
- ✅ Hook (15+ deals analyzed)
- ✅ Why GS specific (leadership complex transactions)
- ✅ ~60 mots (optimal)

**§2 (110 mots):**
> During my internship at Rothschild & Co, I supported the execution of a €450M pharmaceutical M&A, conducting financial modeling (DCF, LBO) and due diligence across 8 European markets. I streamlined the comparables analysis process, reducing preparation time by 30%, which enabled senior bankers to accelerate client presentations. Additionally, in my student investment fund role, I led the analysis of a €2M equity investment in a French fintech, achieving 22% IRR over 18 months through rigorous sector research and active portfolio monitoring.

**Analysis:**
- ✅ 2 achievements quantifiés
- ✅ Metrics: €450M, 30%, €2M, 22% IRR
- ✅ Action verbs: supported, conducted, streamlined, led
- ✅ Technical skills: DCF, LBO, due diligence
- ✅ ~110 mots

**§3 (90 mots):**
> I am particularly motivated by M&A advisory's strategic dimension—helping clients navigate transformative decisions through rigorous analysis and creative structuring. My academic focus on corporate finance and valuation, combined with my cross-cultural fluency (French, English, Mandarin), positions me well for Goldman's international deal teams. I am impressed by Goldman's recent role as advisor on the Sanofi-Translate Bio acquisition, which exemplifies the type of complex, high-stakes work I aspire to contribute to.

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Why M&A (passion motivation)
- ✅ Skills link (valuation, languages)
- ✅ Specific deal mention (Sanofi-Translate Bio)
- ✅ Research signal (recent deal knowledge)
- ✅ ~90 mots

**§4 (60 mots):**
> Goldman Sachs' unparalleled training program and culture of meritocracy align with my commitment to continuous learning and excellence. Conversations with current analysts highlighted the firm's collaborative environment and emphasis on intellectual curiosity—values I demonstrated through my leadership of HEC's Finance Society, organizing 12 industry events with 500+ attendees.

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Why GS not JPM/MS (training, meritocracy)
- ✅ Cultural fit (collaborative, curious)
- ✅ Networking signal (conversations with analysts)
- ✅ Achievement: 12 events, 500+ attendees
- ✅ ~60 mots

**§5 (50 mots):**
> I am available for an interview at your earliest convenience and would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my analytical skills and M&A experience can contribute to Goldman Sachs' continued success. Thank you for your consideration.

> Sincerely,
> [Name]

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Disponibilité
- ✅ CTA clair
- ✅ Remerciements
- ✅ ~50 mots
- **TOTAL: ~370 mots** ✅

## 🎯 Postulae Implementation Guidelines

### Pipeline LLM

**Step 1: Extract Job Requirements (Claude Haiku 3)**
- Input: `job_offer` (texte brut)
- Output: JSON structuré
  ```json
  {
    "company_name": "Goldman Sachs",
    "position": "Investment Banking Analyst",
    "division": "M&A",
    "required_skills": ["Financial modeling", "Due diligence", "M&A"],
    "experience_years": "0-2",
    "sector": "Finance - Investment Banking",
    "key_values": ["Meritocracy", "Client-first", "Intellectual rigor"],
    "key_responsibilities": ["Financial modeling", "Client presentations", "Deal execution"]
  }
  ```

**Step 2: Match CV to Job (Internal Logic)**
- Scan `cv_data.work_experience` pour achievements pertinents
- Identifier 2-3 bullets qui match `required_skills`
- Extraire metrics quantifiés (%, €, deals, team size)
- Vérifier language skills si international role

**Step 3: Generate Cover Letter (Claude Sonnet 4.5)**
- Input: `cv_data` + `job_requirements` + `additional_notes`
- Constraints: 300-350 mots, 4-5 §, structure stricte
- Output: Texte brut français (ou anglais)

**Step 4: Translate (Claude Haiku 3)**
- Si génération FR → traduire EN (ou inverse)
- Adapter formules closing selon langue
- Conserver metrics et noms propres

**Step 5: Layout HTML/CSS**
- Template: `cover_letter_template.html`
- Header réutilisé du CV (nom, contact)
- Body avec paragraphes aérés
- Signature professionnelle

**Step 6: Generate PDF (Playwright)**
- Chromium headless rendering
- A4, marges 20/25mm
- Export PDF + DOCX
